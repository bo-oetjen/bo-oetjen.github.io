UC Command Center

A dashboard that runs my degree: this week's plan synced live with Todoist, a semester calendar, grades with a GPA simulator, an academic advising tracker and statistics on study time versus results.

Problem

Five courses, a full-time job and deadlines spread across Canvas, syllabi, email and Todoist. No single view of what was due, how I was doing, or what to register for next.

Built

An installable web app wired to Todoist, with a shared database for grades, syllabi and advising notes, plus its own backlog and change log.

Result

62 logged changes in the first 10 days. It replaced a weekly planner I rebuilt by hand and became the one place I check each morning.

The full story

The problem, what I built and why, and what's next.

Why I built it

I started with a printable weekly planner generated from Todoist. It worked, but keeping it current was a job in itself. The Command Center makes the planner live and adds the parts of school that had no home: grades, degree requirements and conversations with my advisor.

What it does

  • This Week: tasks grouped by course and ordered by urgency, a "time left today" estimate, and automatic roll-forward of unfinished work that marks genuine overdue items differently from work started early.
  • Calendar: assignments, UC academic dates, federal holidays, class meetings and my work PTO on one month view.
  • Grades & GPA: a grade book per course and a GPA simulator that applies each syllabus's drop and replacement rules, with what-if scores that are never saved over real ones.
  • Academic Advising: requirements remaining, courses I'm eligible to register for, alternative course plans and a log of advisor replies.
  • Statistics: estimated effort by course and week compared with grades, so I can see where time is going.
  • Admin: a backlog with statuses (Planned, In Progress, Not Planned, Done) and a dated change log. I run my own tool the way I'd run a product.

Decisions worth noting

  • Todoist stays the source of truth. The dashboard reads and completes tasks but never rewrites an instructor's due date.
  • Timezone safety: the date is always checked in Eastern time, after an early bug flagged work overdue hours before midnight.
  • Every change is logged, so I can explain why the tool behaves the way it does.
